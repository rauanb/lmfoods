About Us

Bringing the heart of Brazilian flavors to your table

LMFoods was born from a simple desire: to share the rich culinary heritage of Minas Gerais with the world. Nestled in the heart of Brazil, Minas Gerais is a region celebrated for its vibrant food culture, warm hospitality, and flavors that have been cherished for generations — and it's where our family story begins. When we made Calgary our home, we brought that tradition with us. Every sauce we craft carries the same care, the same ingredients, and the same love that have defined our family table for decades. Today, we are proud to serve the Calgary community, sharing a little taste of Brazil with every jar. Welcome to LMFoods — a little piece of Minas Gerais, right here in Calgary.
