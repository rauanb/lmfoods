About Us

Founded in Calgary, Alberta, Lino Macedo Foods - LM FOODS is a family-owned company built on a simple belief: great food has the power to bring people together and create lasting memories.

From the very beginning, our mission has been to craft high-quality products with transparency, care, and a genuine passion for serving others. Every recipe is prepared with love, dedication, and attention to detail, with the goal of delighting our customers through unforgettable flavors.

At the heart of our company is Livia Lino, a nutritionist whose remarkable talent for creating exceptional food has always been more than a skill — it is a true gift. Inspired by her family roots and guided by a passion for nourishing people, she brings authenticity and creativity to every product we make.

Our love for Calgary and for Canada inspired us to turn that passion into a business dedicated to creating premium products for Canadian families. Today, Lino Macedo Foods proudly combines family values, quality ingredients, and a commitment to excellence to deliver products made with heart.

Because for us, food is more than something we eat — it is a way to share love.
